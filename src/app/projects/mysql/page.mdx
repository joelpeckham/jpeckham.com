This series is a set of interactive articles for learning MySQL and InnoDB in depth — aimed at web application programmers who want mental models that survive real traffic, not a surface-level cheat sheet.

**Part A** covers foundations: schema, indexes, queries, writes, and transactions. **Part B** goes deeper into MVCC, locks, the buffer pool, durability, migrations, replication, and forensics.

Each topic is its own post. Start at the top and move through in order, or jump to whatever you need. Prev/next links at the bottom of each post keep you in the series.
